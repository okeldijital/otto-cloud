## Decision

The **Royalty Entitlement Domain** defines **financial participation**, not monetary calculation.

| Domain | Owns |
|--------|------|
| Contracts | Legal evidence |
| Rights | Legal operations |
| **Royalty Entitlements** | **Who is paid, for what, where, when** |
| Future Usage | Consumption data |
| Future Calculation | Monetary computation |

Entitlements are promoted only from **approved/active Rights**. Human review is required. No automatic publication.
